Overview

    Where to look next

    Accounts and hashtags below come only from your own saved videos. The ad libraries are fixed public sources.

    Research projects

    A project is a set of saved videos for one product or question. For each project: accounts and videos to look at next, keywords and products, what to make more of, a remix clip bank and an AI plan. Pick a project in the Analyze bar (Collections menu) to see every other tab for just that project.

    Add videos fromor tick videos in the Library tab.

    Creators

    Every creator you saved, with the numbers of the videos you saved from them. Click a column to sort, a name to see their videos. “Lift” = their median views ÷ the median of everything in this view. Instagram hides views, so there it compares likes (marked “likes”).

    Formats

    Content type is read from words in the title, caption and hashtags (for example “how to”, “vs”, “before and after”, “POV”, “#ad”). A video can have several. Lift above 1.0x means that format gets more views than your typical saved video.

    AI tags (optional, uses your own key)

    The model labels each video's hook type, angle, audience and emotion from its title, caption and transcript (transcribe videos first for better labels). Lift then shows which choices win in this view. Sends at most 40 untagged videos per click to api.anthropic.com.

    Keywords

    Words and hashtags that appear in 2 or more of your saved videos, with how those videos perform. Click a keyword to see its videos.

    Creative brief

    Built from the videos in the current Analyze view. Each line says what it is based on.

    AI assistant (optional, uses your own key)

    Uses your own AI API key (set it in Settings). When you press a button, a capped summary of your library (titles, creators, hashtags, first caption line, view and like counts) is sent to api.anthropic.com. Nothing is sent otherwise.

      Features

      Downloading is always on. Everything else can be switched off; switched-off features disappear from the dashboard and run nothing in the background.

      AI API key and cost

      Your Anthropic API key (console.anthropic.com → API keys), stored only in this browser. AI buttons send a capped summary of the chosen videos to api.anthropic.com; each call is billed to your key (model claude-sonnet-5-5, $2 / $10 per million input / output tokens).

      0 = no limit. A call that could go over the limit is refused.

      Ad Studio

      Ad Studio (your separate app on this Mac) makes the ads: the extension hands it the saved reference video, the product link and the plan, or cuts remix-bank clips into its library. Nothing is rebuilt here.
