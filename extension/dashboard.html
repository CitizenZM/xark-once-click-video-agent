Overview

    Where to look next

    Accounts and hashtags below come only from your own saved videos. The ad libraries are fixed public sources.

    Creators

    Every creator you saved, with the numbers of the videos you saved from them. Click a column to sort, a name to see their videos. “Lift” = their median views ÷ the median of everything in this view. Instagram hides views, so there it compares likes (marked “likes”).

    Formats

    Content type is read from words in the title, caption and hashtags (for example “how to”, “vs”, “before and after”, “POV”, “#ad”). A video can have several. Lift above 1.0x means that format gets more views than your typical saved video.

    AI tags (optional, uses your own key)

    The model labels each video's hook type, angle, audience and emotion from its title, caption and transcript (transcribe videos first for better labels). Lift then shows which choices win in this view. Sends at most 40 untagged videos per click to api.anthropic.com.

    Keywords

    Words and hashtags that appear in 2 or more of your saved videos, with how those videos perform. Click a keyword to see its videos.

    Creative brief

    Built from the videos in the current Analyze view. Each line says what it is based on.

    AI assistant (optional, uses your own key)

    Optional. Uses your own Anthropic API key, stored only in this browser. When you press a button, a capped summary of your library (titles, creators, hashtags, first caption line, view and like counts) is sent to api.anthropic.com. Nothing is sent otherwise.
