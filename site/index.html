Xark Once Click Video Agent

Save the TikTok, Instagram or YouTube video you like in one click. Pick videos on a page, keep them in collections, and let AI point out what to study next.

Download the extension (.zip) GitHub

What it does

One click

Click the icon on a video page, or press Alt+Shift+D. The file lands in your Downloads folder.

Select on a page

Tick videos on a feed or profile, or Select all, then download them as a queue.

Collections

Folders for your saved video links. Move in bulk, filter, copy a collection's links.

Dashboard

Library, charts, outliers, creators and hashtags worth exploring, plus fixed public ad libraries.

AI assistant (optional)

With your own Anthropic key: recommendations from your library and auto-sort into collections, only when you press the button.

Local and private

Downloads run on your computer with engine. No accounts, no analytics.

Install in 2 steps

  1. Install the helper (macOS or Linux). It sets up engine and ffmpeg and registers a small local program that Chrome talks to. 
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CitizenZM/xark-once-click-video-agent/main/install.sh | bash
    Windows: an experimental installer is in the GitHub repo (install.ps1), not yet tested on a real PC.
  2. Add the extension. Download the zip above and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin the icon.

Then click the icon → Dashboard. It should say “Helper ready”. Use the zip from this page: it keeps the extension ID the helper expects.

Good to know

  • You are responsible for respecting each platform's terms and the creators' rights. Save references for research; do not republish other people's videos.
  • Instagram sometimes needs your Chrome login cookies; the helper reads them locally only when an anonymous try fails.
  • The AI buttons send a capped summary of your saved videos to api.anthropic.com with your key. Nothing is sent otherwise. Privacy.
